## Main screen ray

On iPhone the live ray fills the gap between the quip and the amount. `RaynaStage.side` is 90% of that slot's width, or the slot height when that is smaller, so a short phone shrinks her instead of clipping. She is drawn square (the art is square) and aligned to the bottom of the gap, over the water. The quip is at most three lines at normal type sizes, and the column scrolls at accessibility sizes with the ray still at 90% of the width. iPad uses the same rule in the left column. The Mac panel is 380×740 with a ray about 310pt tall. The Watch sizes her from the screen, leaving room for the litres and the + button.

The exercise-day switch is not on this screen. It lives in Settings → Exercise days (see Main screen layouts). `RaynaCopy.runDayLabel` still reads **Exercise day (+500 ml)**, with `dumbbell.fill`, for any leftover label. A build that still says "Run day" has not picked up this branch.

## Status lines

The line under the title, the reminders, the exercise-day lines and the kid-mode lines live in `Sources/RaynaCore/QuipLines.swift`. There are 30 for each progress band (empty, ripple, glide, halfway, rising, almost), and 30 each for the goal, reminders, exercise day and kid mode.

Every line is written for children as well as adults: no innuendo, alcohol, body-shaming, diet talk or medical claims. Exercise-day lines say exercise, workout, moving, playing or swimming. A shuffle bag per list means the line just shown is not the next one. A new glass, a new band, or a new part of the day draws the next line. A redraw keeps the current one. Some lines are tagged morning, afternoon, evening or night. The rest fit any hour.

Kid mode has no separate censored list, because the whole catalog is kid-safe. On an in-progress band it uses that band plus the kid-mode lines. The goal still uses the goal lines. Kid mode still has no exercise day. Reminders use the reminder list, including on the scheduled notifications, and stay fixed text.

## Your own invite link

Before accepting a link, Rayna fetches the share metadata. If `participantRole` is owner, or the share's owner record is the current user, it does not accept. It opens the pod and shows "That's your own pod invite — send it to a friend to have them join!" If CloudKit still rejects the accept because the owner tried to join, that error is caught and treated the same way. Someone who has already accepted the invite opens the pod instead of seeing an error. Network, sign-in, storage, a cancelled invite, and a missing zone each have their own sentence. The red line never includes a URL or the raw CloudKit string. Debug builds print that string and, when the debug panel is on, show it there.

## Pending pod invites

- On the pod screen (iOS Settings → Pod, the pod sheet, and the Mac Journey pod card), read `CKShare.participants` on the pod share in zone `RaynaPod`.
- Participants with `acceptanceStatus == .pending` (not the owner, not the current user) render as greyed tiles beside member tiles: faded stingray, name from `nameComponents`, then email, then phone, or **Invited** if all are empty, and the line **Waiting to join**.
- Accepted participants are not tiles of their own. Once they have a `Member` record they show as a normal tank tile.
- Tap a pending tile (owner only): **Resend invite** presents `UICloudSharingController` (iOS) or `NSSharingService.cloudSharing` (Mac) for the existing share. **Cancel invite** asks for confirmation, then `CKShare.removeParticipant` and save. Anyone else can see the tile but not change it.
- The Invite button uses that same share sheet when the app has wired a presenter, so named invites actually become pending participants. Public-link joins still skip the pending state until the person is added by name.
- No new CloudKit record type or field. Participants live on the existing `CKShare`.

## Custom glass size

- The same control on iPhone, Mac Settings, the menu-bar panel and the Welcome screen: `GlassSizePicker`.
- The picker lists 150 / 200 / 250 / 330 / 400 / 500 ml, plus **Custom**.
- The millilitre field appears only while Custom is selected. Choosing a preset hides the field and saves that preset. If the saved `glassML` is not a preset, the picker opens on Custom and the field shows that value.
- Valid range is **50–2000 ml** (whole millilitres). The field rejects anything else and leaves the saved size unchanged. Stored values outside the range are clamped into it (0 stays the 250 ml default). The Custom row uses a tag that is never stored.
- Saved in the existing `glassML` preference. iCloud sync already ships that key inside the `RaynaPrefs` JSON `payload` (last-writer-wins). No new record field.
- +1 glass on iPhone, Mac menu bar and Apple Watch uses `glassML`. Watch has no editor; the iPhone pushes the size over WatchConnectivity. Quick add on iPhone and Mac can change the size without opening the rest of Settings.

## Wear up to 3 badges

Each badge has one slot in `RaynaUnlock.slot`: **Head** (workout headband, the 30-day crown, spring tide), **Chest** (workout medal, the 7-day rosette, the 3-day stone) or **Fin** (bubble trail, baby ray, ripple, flood, personal best, pod wave). Rayna wears at most one badge in each slot. Putting another badge in a full slot swaps it. Taking one off leaves that slot empty.

The trophy screen (iPhone Badges sheet and the Mac Badges window) opens with a "Ray is wearing" card: Rayna with the worn badges, and a Head / Chest / Fin row. A filled row shows the badge icon and an x. An empty row is a dashed outline labelled Empty. The grid is two columns. VoiceOver says Worn or Not worn, and the slot name.

On the main screen each worn badge is drawn at its slot, scaled with Rayna: on the head above her eyes, on the chest below the smile, and by her right fin. The imageset is cropped to the badge itself, so a wide scene does not cover her face. A missing imageset falls back to a symbol at that same slot. ## What was slow, and why

A glass logged on one device could sit for minutes on the others. The merge rules were not the cause, and they are unchanged.

- **The upload waited.** `syncSoon` held the private-zone save for two seconds after the last tap. It now waits at most one second, and only to collapse a burst of taps. It does not wait for a timer or for the app to go to the background. The pod member record is written on each log. See "Your own pod tile" below.
- **The Mac almost never asked.** A five-minute timer was the only regular fetch, and it fetched the private log only, not the pod. There was no fetch when the menu-bar panel opened, when the app became active, or when the Mac woke from sleep. Those three now fetch both zones, and the timer is one minute. iPhone already fetched the private log on foreground; it now fetches the pod as well.
- **Pushes were easy to miss.** A silent `CKDatabaseSubscription` for the private database was saved once, the first time the RaynaSync zone was created, and a failure was ignored. The pod subscription was saved only inside Start pod / Accept invite, so a later launch of an existing pod did not subscribe. The shared database had a subscription only for someone who had just accepted. There was no `CKRecordZoneSubscription`. Launch now saves, and retries until it succeeds: a silent database subscription on the private database and on the shared database (when you are in a pod as a participant), plus a zone subscription for `RaynaSync` and for `RaynaPod` on whichever database that zone lives in. `shouldSendContentAvailable` is set and there is no alert. iPhone and Mac already call `registerForRemoteNotifications`. `didReceiveRemoteNotification` fetches with the stored change token.
- **The pod re-read its history.** Every pod fetch walked the zone from the beginning. It now keeps the server change token and applies the delta to the roster it already has. A missing or expired token reads the zone once from the start, then stores the new token.
- **The Watch path was already the right shape.** A tap uses `sendMessage` while the phone is reachable and `transferUserInfo` otherwise. Taps that happen before the session is active are held and sent the same way when activation finishes. The phone still applies them into the same log, which then uploads within a second. While the phone is reachable it also pushes the latest totals back with `sendMessage`, and falls back to the application context.

No new CloudKit record types or fields. Subscriptions are per-user push registrations, not schema, so **Production does not need a schema deploy**. The app creates them the first time that build runs in each environment (Development and Production are separate). Nothing has to be added by hand in the CloudKit Console.

Entitlements already in the project, which Nik does not need to add again:

- iPhone: `aps-environment` = `development` (Xcode sets `production` on archive) and `UIBackgroundModes` includes `remote-notification`.
- Mac: `com.apple.developer.aps-environment` = `production`.

One thing to know while testing from Xcode: a Development iPhone uses the APNs sandbox, and the Mac entitlement is production. Silent pushes may not cross those two. The data is still in the same CloudKit container. Opening the Mac menu, bringing the iPhone forward, waking the Mac, or the one-minute poll still fetches. A TestFlight or App Store build of both uses production APNs, so the push is the fast path there. The App ID already needs Push Notifications and iCloud for the container `iCloud.co.versantus.raynawater`; that is existing setup, not a new capability.

## Your own pod tile

Nik's tile ("Bob (you)") read the shared Member record and captioned it as a glass count plus a cup emoji (`0 🥛 · 0%`). The main screen counts millilitres (`1.00 L` of `3.2 L`, 31%). Those are different units, and the record was still the zeros written when the pod was created: `ml` 0, `glasses` 0, and an `updated` time that is not today, which forces the tank to empty. On iPhone the write was also skipped until the pod screen had already checked iCloud, so a glass logged before that never reached the zone. A new day zeroed the phone and the Mac locally and left yesterday's Member record in place.

The local tile now uses today's millilitres and goal from this device. `+1` and undo repaint it immediately, without waiting for CloudKit. The caption matches the main screen, for example `1.00 L · 31%`. Someone who has not synced today still shows `0.00 L · 0%` and an empty tank. The same `PodView` is the iPhone, iPad and Mac pod screen, and the compact "Your pod" bars use the same numbers.

Each log, undo and day change writes `ml`, `goal`, `glasses`, `streak` and `updated` on the existing Member record (`updated` is the day). The iPhone also writes when it comes to the foreground, so a day the widget already rolled still reaches the pod. The Mac writes on wake and when it becomes active. Opening the pod screen writes again. Numbers are read back from CloudKit as `NSNumber` or `Double` as well as `Int`, so a real total is not shown as 0.

## Each pod tile's Ray

Pod tiles all called `RaynaStill(progress:)`, which paints the signed-in colour (`RaynaFrames.skin`). Two people on the same progress band (Nik at 42% and Sophie at 43% are both the "fine" still) therefore showed the same ray: Sophie's tile was Nik's Coral ray. Worn badges were never on the Member record, so they could not appear on anyone else's tile.

The look is the same two values the Badges screen already stores, written on the shared `Member` record:

- `raynaSkin` (STRING): `classic`, `coral`, `mint`, and the pack colours when someone is wearing one.
- `wornSet` (INT64): the same bit mask as the prefs payload. Head, chest and fin badges are drawn on that tile. A scene stays in the water on the main screen; the pod tank keeps its own water.

A missing field, or a colour name this app does not know, is the classic turquoise ray with nothing worn, so an older app in the pod still has a tile. The signed-in tile does not use that record for its picture: it keeps the still the rest of the app paints (`RaynaFrames.skin`) and the badges from this device. Changing colour or badges publishes with the next pod write (a glass, opening the pod, or the change itself on iPhone and Mac).

Development creates the two fields the first time this build saves a Member record. Production does not. Deploy the schema before a TestFlight build that writes them.

### Nudges and free text

Pod nudges cannot contain typed text. `PodService.nudge` writes a `Nudge` record with `to`, `from`, `fromName` and `created` only. The notification body is always `"{name} nudged you: time for a glass of water! 🌊"`. Reminder nudges use the fixed lines in `QuipLines` (`RaynaCopy.reminders`). Fizzy-drink nudges use `swapText`. The pod name and display name are names, not messages. There is no chat and no free-text messaging between people.
