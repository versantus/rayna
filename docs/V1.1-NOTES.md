## Pending pod invites

- On the pod screen (iOS Settings → Pod, the pod sheet, and the Mac Journey pod card), read `CKShare.participants` on the pod share in zone `RaynaPod`.
- Participants with `acceptanceStatus == .pending` (not the owner, not the current user) render as greyed tiles beside member tiles: faded stingray, name from `nameComponents`, then email, then phone, or **Invited** if all are empty, and the line **Waiting to join**.
- Accepted participants are not tiles of their own. Once they have a `Member` record they show as a normal tank tile.
- Tap a pending tile (owner only): **Resend invite** presents `UICloudSharingController` (iOS) or `NSSharingService.cloudSharing` (Mac) for the existing share. **Cancel invite** asks for confirmation, then `CKShare.removeParticipant` and save. Anyone else can see the tile but not change it.
- The Invite button uses that same share sheet when the app has wired a presenter, so named invites actually become pending participants. Public-link joins still skip the pending state until the person is added by name.
- No new CloudKit record type or field. Participants live on the existing `CKShare`.

## Custom glass size

- Keep the existing presets: iOS picker 150 / 200 / 250 / 330 / 400 / 500 ml; Mac stepper 100–750 ml in steps of 50.
- Also let someone type a size. Valid range is **50–2000 ml** (whole millilitres). The field rejects anything else and leaves the saved size unchanged. Stored values outside the range are clamped into it (0 stays the 250 ml default).
- Saved in the existing `glassML` preference. iCloud sync already ships that key inside the `RaynaPrefs` JSON `payload` (last-writer-wins). No new record field.
- +1 glass on iPhone, Mac menu bar and Apple Watch uses `glassML`. Watch has no editor; the iPhone pushes the size over WatchConnectivity. Quick add on iPhone and Mac can change the size without opening the rest of Settings.

## Over-achiever badges

Same `RaynaUnlock` collection, celebration card and iCloud profile union (`unlocks` strings inside `RaynaProfile.payload`). `noteStreak` still awards only the original streak badges.

| id | earned when | art | symbol fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `surge` | 125% of today's goal in a day | ripple rings and droplets where her tail meets the water | `bolt.fill` |
| `tide` | 150% of today's goal | full moon, rising swell, up arrows | `drop.fill` |
| `flood` | 200% of today's goal | water to mid-tail, bubbles, gold and white sparkles | `sparkles` |
| `over3` | beat the goal 3 days in a row, ending on a day that itself beat it | milestone stone marked 3, red flag, dotted swim trail | `flame` |
| `over7` | 7 days | blue rosette with a 7 and red ribbon tails | `flame.fill` |
| `over30` | 30 days | gold crown with gems, where the headband sits | `crown.fill` |
| `personalBest` | today is strictly more than any earlier logged day (the first day does not count) | gold trophy marked PB on her disc, with confetti | `trophy.fill` |
| `podWave` | two or more pod members, each with today's `ml` strictly above their own `goal` | Rayna plus four pod rays above a waterline | `person.3.fill` |

- "Beat the goal" is `ml > goal`, not merely meeting it. Today's goal is the effective goal (run-day bonus included on iPhone). Earlier days in the over-streak are compared with today's goal, because history stores millilitres only.
- Pod-wide uses existing `Member.ml` and `Member.goal`. Progress shown in the tank stays capped at 100%; the badge uses the raw numbers.
- Badges are not revoked if a later undo drops the total.
- Final art is in `iOS/Rayna/Assets.xcassets` and `iOS/RaynaMac/Assets.xcassets`. Each badge has two universal 512×512 imagesets: `badge-<id>` is the band3 still with the badge baked in (the collection cell uses this as one image), and `badge-<id>-overlay` is the badge alone on a transparent square. Celebration, the live ray and the share card lay the overlay over Rayna the same way the code-drawn accessories do (`ZStack` + `scaledToFit`), including several at once. Watch and the widgets do not show badges, so those catalogs are unchanged.
- If an imageset is missing, a single badge falls back to the SF Symbol in the table. The menu-bar app built with `./build.sh` has no asset catalog, so it keeps that fallback. The Xcode **RaynaMac** scheme uses `iOS/RaynaMac/Assets.xcassets` and shows the art.
- Re-render from `tools/badge-art/` (`python3 build.py`). Needs `skia-python`, `pillow`, and the Nunito variable font (`RAYNA_FONT`, or the path in `draw.py`). The background still is `Sources/RaynaCore/Resources/RaynaFrames/stills/band3.png`. Output lands in `tools/badge-art/out/` and is not committed; copy the imagesets into both catalogs above to ship a new render.
- Debug builds can unlock without earning the day. iOS Settings and Mac Settings have **Unlock all badges**. Launch either app with `RAYNA_BADGES=all`, or a comma-separated list of ids. On the simulator that is `SIMCTL_CHILD_RAYNA_BADGES=all`. This writes the same unlock list a real award writes, including the iCloud profile union.
