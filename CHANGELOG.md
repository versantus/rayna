# Changelog

All notable changes to Rayna (macOS menu-bar app + iOS app). macOS versions are listed; the iOS app ships as 1.0 (unreleased).

## Unreleased
- Pod tiles show each member's own Ray. The tank was painting every still with the signed-in colour, so two people on the same progress band (for example 42% and 43%) shared one ray. Each Member record now carries `raynaSkin` and `wornSet` (the same colour and worn badges as the Badges screen). A friend's tile is recoloured from that, with their head, chest and fin badges. A record without those fields (an older app) shows the classic ray. Your own tile still uses the still the rest of the app paints. Deploy the Member fields to the Production CloudKit schema before a TestFlight build that writes them.
- Released: Rayna's free colours (Turquoise, Coral, Mint) in a new Colours tab on the Badges screen, synced across iPhone, iPad, Mac, widgets and Watch. Still hidden until the pack can be bought: the premium scenes and the paid colours (`RaynaFeatures.scenePack` / `paidColours`). Labs only counts in Debug and TestFlight builds (`labsAllowed`), so an App Store install ignores a switch left on from testing.
- Scene creatures move their limbs: each has a 5-second swim cycle from Higgsfield (turtle flippers stroke, fish tails swish, jellies pulse, the octopus curls its arms, the seahorse's fin flutters, baby rays ripple), 40 frames at 180 px in `RaynaFrames/scene/anim/<loop>/` (6.4 MB), made with `tools/rayna-frames/loops.py`. Each creature plays at its own offset so they're never in step; babies and look-alikes share a loop. The Watch keeps stills to save space. Applies to the free scenes too (fish, baby rays).
- Not released (behind `RaynaFeatures.scenePack` and `RaynaFeatures.rayColours`, both off; Settings → Labs previews them in Debug and TestFlight builds only):
  - The scene & colour pack: Coral Reef, Moon Jellies, Sunken Treasure and Turtle Bay. Higgsfield art (seabed backdrops and creatures, jellies cut from black so they glow), drawn in layers with code effects: light shafts, bubbles, twinkles, glowing plankton, dusk water. Every creature moves on its own uneven rhythm (fish meander and turn, a pair of clownfish swim together, jellies rise and pulse, an octopus peeks out and hides, a shoal passes, turtles glide with a stroke), so the scenes don't feel like a short loop. Shared layout and drawing (`SeaSceneLayout.swift`, `SeaSceneDraw.swift`) so the Watch shows them too.
  - Rayna's colours: Coral and Mint free, Sunshine, Lilac, Ocean and Midnight in the pack. A gradient map over her turquoise body only (eyes, blush, smile and rim untouched), applied as a colour cube while frames decode: no extra download. Stored as `raynaSkin` in the existing prefs payload; the Watch uses pre-coloured stills (`band<n>-<colour>`). `tools/rayna-frames/recolour.py` is the same maths for previews.
  - Debug launch settings `RAYNA_LABS=1` and `RAYNA_SKIN=<colour>`.
- Watch sync is faster: the watch asks the phone for today's numbers as soon as it opens (a "hello" message, answered in milliseconds) and shows "Updating…" until they arrive; the phone sends the moment the watch app comes into reach; and with the watch app closed, the watch face gets the fast complication channel when watchOS has budget left. The phone no longer re-sends identical numbers after every change, and coalesces a burst of changes into one send.
- Testing: "Reset today's nudge limits" on the pod screen clears this phone's 3-per-person nudge counts and Sent ticks for today. Shown only in Debug and TestFlight builds (StoreKit `AppTransaction` sandbox environment), never in the App Store build.
- Apple Watch: Rayna now installs on watchOS 10 and later. The project never set a watchOS minimum, so Xcode used its newest (27.0) and Watches on older watchOS (e.g. 10.6) couldn't install it.
- Nudges are visible inside Rayna, not only as a notification: a banner drops in ("Sophie nudged you. Time for a glass!") and Rayna wiggles; the pod screen lists "Nudged you today" with times; and the sender sees "Sent ✓ 14:32" once the nudge has saved to iCloud. Debug launch setting `RAYNA_NUDGE=<name>` fakes one.
- Pods: people who joined from an invite never got nudges pushed to them. The shared database only accepts database subscriptions, and asking for a zone subscription too failed the whole save; the zone one is now only made in the owner's private database. Starting or joining a pod asks for notification permission (nudges are notifications).
- Pods: someone who joined from a link showed as "Me". Your name now defaults to your iCloud first name when you join, "Your name in the pod" can be changed on the pod screen at any time (published straight away), and older tiles saved as "Me" show the person's iCloud name from the share.
- Scenes have their own tab on the Badges screen, with two free scenes from the start: Seabed (shells and a starfish) and Seagrass meadow (low swaying seagrass). Earned scenes keep their rules. Free scenes are never worn automatically. New badges are appended, so every older badge keeps its code in `wornSet`. Debug launch settings `RAYNA_WEAR` and `RAYNA_JOURNEY_TAB=scenes`.
- The percent no longer floats on the waterline, where it collided with the quip, the header buttons and (on Balanced) the ring, worst at 100%. Each layout shows it in one fixed place: next to the litres on Hero and Balanced (Balanced also keeps its ring, and the quip now stops short of it), and in the title on Immersive ("of 2.5 L goal · 60%"). Debug launch setting `RAYNA_LAYOUT=immersive|hero|balanced` for screenshots.
- Ready for iPhone Duo (and resizable iPhone Mirroring): the short-screen spacing now follows the window's height (`raynaWindowHeight`, from `onGeometryChange`) instead of `UIScreen.main`, so folding or unfolding mid-session lays the screen out again. Built with the iOS 27.1 SDK, so the app fills both displays; layouts already choose by available space, not device or orientation.
- Apple Watch is immersive: the whole screen is the sea, rising with today's progress, with Rayna floating in it (waves and bubbles pause when the watch dims). +250 ml and a new Undo sit together at the bottom; Undo takes back the last glass on the phone (`undoGlass`), and a + and Undo not yet sent cancel out. The scene worn on the phone appears in the watch sea (`scene` in the watch context), drawn with the same `SeaSceneLayout` file the iPhone uses (compact: smaller moon beside the litres, seabed things at the edges). The watch still takes its goal (with any exercise-day bonus) and glass size from the phone.
- iPad held upright uses the iPhone layouts (Immersive, Hero, Balanced, and the press-and-hold picker); landscape keeps the two columns.
- The 30-day baby ray buddy is one of the new baby-ray sprites instead of the old flat drawing.
- New App Store screenshots for iPhone 6.9" and 6.3", 13" iPad and Apple Watch, made with `iOS/scripts/store-shots.sh` (needs a signed Debug build; an unsigned one has no iCloud entitlement and crashes at launch). Debug launch settings `RAYNA_BANNER=0` (no unlock card) and `RAYNA_LAYOUTS=1` (open the layout picker) for screenshots.
- Press and hold the iPhone main screen to choose a layout: the screen shrinks to live cards of Immersive, Hero and Balanced; swipe between them and tap one (or Done). It saves `mainLayout` the same way Settings does, so it syncs. VoiceOver has a "Change layout" action. iPad keeps its two-column screen.
- Badges move with Rayna. Head, chest and fin badges follow her head, chest and right wing in every clip and still, using per-frame poses in `RaynaFrames/anchors/` made by `tools/rayna-frames/track.py`. They fade out as she turns to swim off and back in as she arrives. The fin slot is now on her right wing (it used to float in the water by her tail). The personal-best overlay is trimmed to the trophy so it is a readable size (`tools/badge-art/trim.py`).
- New Scene slot: Ripple, Spring tide, Flood tide and Pod wave live in the water around her instead of on her body. One scene can be worn alongside a head, chest and fin badge. No new CloudKit fields (worn badges are still the `wornSet` bit mask; slots are derived from the badge).
- Scenes are life in the existing ocean, not water of their own, and they animate (still with Reduce Motion): Ripple is a shoal of fish crossing the water, Spring tide a glowing moon in the sky with shells and a starfish on the sand, Flood tide seagrass and kelp swaying from the seabed, Pod wave baby rays drifting around her. They follow the water level: plants never grow above the water, swimmers stay under the wave (fewer and smaller when it is shallow), and the moon needs sky. Drawn behind Rayna on Immersive, the Hero/Balanced water card, iPad and the Mac panel; collection cards show a small aquarium. Art from Higgsfield in `RaynaFrames/scene`, cut with `tools/rayna-frames/sprites.py`; layout and motion in `SeaScene.swift`.
- Fixed: Hero and Balanced left a large gap at the bottom. The water card kept its natural proportions (`.aspectRatio(nil, ...)` is not "no ratio") instead of filling the screen.
- Fixed: badge art was drawn upside down wherever it was cropped to the badge (trophy, crown, rosette, milestone stone).
- Below 45% Rayna is now the same turquoise ray as in every other clip, just sleepy (heavy eyelids, wobbly mouth, slow wing ripple and a blink). Before, the thirsty loop was a different grey render, so +1 glass swapped characters. New `idle-thirsty` loop (62 frames) and `band0`/`band1` stills, also in the Watch catalogs. Made in Higgsfield from the band2 still; `tools/rayna-frames/key.py` cuts the magenta out and matches framing and colour to `idle-happy`. The `band3` (happy, wings lifted) and `band4` (goal, eyes closed in joy, wings high) stills are redrawn from the same reference, so all five stills share one body, tail and size; the goal still no longer has bubbles or a curled tail. The Mac nudge's `swim-off` clip is regenerated too (46 frames, ending on an empty frame), so she stays the cartoon ray while she turns and leaves instead of becoming a paler, realistic one; `KEY=auto` in the script handles the clip's drifting background colour.
- Rayna can wear up to three badges at once, one each on her head, chest and fin. A new badge is put on when that slot is empty. The Badges screen matches the trophy mockup: a wearing strip, a shirt icon on worn cards, and a short swap toast. On the main screen each badge sits on its slot. The set is `wornSet` inside the existing prefs payload. A missing key still follows the old single `wornBadge`.
- Three iPhone main screens, chosen in Settings → Appearance: Immersive (the default), Hero and Balanced. Rayna fills the card or the ocean. The exercise-day switch has left the main screen. Settings → Exercise days holds the extra water, today's switch, and Apple Health auto-detect. The Mac menu bar drops the switch and grows Rayna; Mac Settings has the same exercise section, without Health. The choice is `mainLayout` inside the existing RaynaPrefs payload.
- Opening your own pod invite no longer shows a raw iCloud error. Rayna opens the pod and says to send the link to a friend. Other CloudKit failures on that screen use a short friendly sentence. The raw error, including any share URL, is only in the debug panel.
- The live ray is sized from the space she is given (`RaynaStage`, 90% of that width, or the height when the slot is shorter). The three iPhone layouts and the Mac menu-bar panel use that. The Watch face uses the same sizing.
- Status lines: 30 variants for each progress band, plus goal, reminder, exercise-day and kid-mode lines (300 in `QuipLines.swift`). A shuffle bag avoids repeating the line just shown, and some lines follow the time of day. Every line is child-friendly, so kid mode can use the whole catalog. Exercise lines talk about exercise, workouts, moving, playing and swimming.
- The live ray wears one badge. By default that is the latest one earned. In the collection, tap a badge and choose Wear, or Wear nothing. The choice is `wornBadge` in the existing prefs payload. Tide, flood and the pod wave do not stack on the headband, medal or bubbles. The celebration card shows the badge just earned, and the share card shows the worn one.
- Debug builds can show sample pod members (not saved to iCloud), a pod debug row (share link, participants, last fetch, subscription) and Refresh pod now. Real two-account steps are in docs/TESTING-PODS.md.
- Your own pod tile follows this device's log. It shows today's litres and percent, the same way as the main screen (`1.00 L · 31%`), and the tank fills as soon as you add or undo a glass. Each log, undo and new day writes `ml`, `goal` and `updated` on the existing Member record so the rest of the pod can see it. No new CloudKit fields.
- Pod screen shows pending CKShare invites as faded tiles (name or "Invited", "Waiting to join"). The owner can resend the share sheet or cancel the invite after a confirmation. No new CloudKit fields.
- Custom glass size: presets stay, and a typed size from 50 to 2,000 ml is saved in `glassML` (already synced on `RaynaPrefs`). +1 glass on iPhone, Mac and Watch uses it. The picker adds a Custom row; the millilitre field appears only while Custom is selected, and a saved size that is not a preset opens on Custom.
- Exercise day: the +500 ml boost is labelled "Exercise day (+500 ml)" (longer copy says "heavy exercise day"), with a dumbbell instead of a running figure. Badge names are Workout headband and Workout medal. Stored keys (`runDay`, `runBonusML`, `healthRunDay`, `healthAutoRun`, `liveActivityRunDay`) and unlock ids (`headband`, `medal`) are unchanged. Apple Health counts heavy-exercise workouts, not only runs. Fixes #6.
- Over-achiever badges: 125/150/200% of a day's goal, 3/7/30-day streaks of beating the goal, a personal-best day, and a pod-wide day where everyone beats their goal. Final art is in the iOS and Mac asset catalogs (`badge-<id>` and `badge-<id>-overlay`); SF Symbols remain only when an asset is missing. Drawing scripts live in `tools/badge-art/`. Debug builds can unlock them with `RAYNA_BADGES` or Settings → Unlock all badges.
- Mac menu bar matches the iPhone: same glass presets, pod screen, streak dots, reminder intervals, share-card preview, settings order and labels. Kid mode uses the same flag. No new CloudKit fields. Pod nudges stay fixed text. See the parity table in docs/V1.1-NOTES.md.
- iCloud sync uploads within a second of a log, and the other device picks it up from a silent push, from coming to the foreground, from opening the Mac menu, or within a minute if a push is missed. Merge rules are unchanged. See docs/V1.1-NOTES.md.

## 2.2.0: 2026-10-10

### Phase D
- **Apple Watch app (watchOS 10+):** big "+1 glass", progress ring, Rayna still by band, synced with the iPhone via WatchConnectivity (application context + messages/queued userInfo). Watch complication (circular/rectangular/corner/inline).
- **Run-day Live Activity (optional toggle):** Lock Screen + Dynamic Island progress with a +1 button. Starts when run day is on, ends at goal or when run day is turned off.

### Phase C
- **Pods (CloudKit sharing, container iCloud.co.versantus.raynawater):** "Start a pod" creates a zone + root record + CKShare (anyone with the link can join). Members publish daily glasses/ml/goal/streak. The shared tank shows everyone's Rayna at their own water level, with "Nudge 👋" (max 3 per person per day; delivered as a local notification after a silent CKDatabaseSubscription push) and a pod streak when everyone hits their goal. Works on iOS and macOS; on macOS it needs an iCloud-entitled signed build (the ad-hoc DMG shows a notice instead).

### Phase B
- **Share cards:** 1080×1920 story and 1080×1080 square cards (Rayna celebrating, stats, rayna-water.netlify.app), rendered with ImageRenderer. Offered on goal, from unlock celebrations and from the weekly recap (streak card). iOS uses ShareLink; macOS uses NSSharingServicePicker. Video share skipped.
- **Apple Health (iOS):** optional auto run day from today's running workouts (+500 ml, +250 ml per extra 30 min past an hour, max +1,000 ml), and optional Dietary Water logging (Undo removes it). Only the permissions for enabled options are requested. Garmin isn't connected directly; it syncs through Apple Health.

### Phase A
- **Rayna evolves:** streak unlocks drawn in code over the animated Rayna: 3 days bubble trail 🫧, 7 days run-day headband 🎽, 14 days race medal 🏅, 30 days baby ray buddy 🐣. Unlock celebration card with confetti, plus a collection screen ("Rayna's journey").
- **Swap-a-pint:** optional evening challenge after 18:00. "I swapped a drink for water" logs a swap and a glass, with its own streak and stats and gentle copy. Toggle in Settings. The macOS evening nudge now has "I swapped one 💧" / "Not tonight".
- **Weekly recap:** 7-day chart, total, daily average, best day, goals hit, streak/best streak, swaps, bathtub and paddling-pool fun facts.
- **iOS Lock Screen widgets:** accessoryCircular (ring), accessoryRectangular (Rayna + progress), accessoryInline.
- Goal quip now matches the new spin ("Happy spin deployed").
