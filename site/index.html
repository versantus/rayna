Rayna

Stay hydrated. Stingrays are happy.

A free little macOS menu-bar app. Rayna fills up as you drink and swims in with a friendly nudge when it's time for another glass.

What Rayna does

💧
Menu-bar fillA drop icon and today's percentage, always in view.
🌊
Animated nudgesRayna swims in with a speech bubble: “Drank one 💧” or “Later”.
🥤
One-click logging+1 glass with undo. Set your own glass size and daily goal.
🏃
Run-day bonusFlip a switch for +500 ml on days you run.
🌙
Quiet hoursNo nudges overnight (22:00–07:30 by default, adjustable).
🥤
Fizzy-drink swapAn optional, guilt-free nudge after 18:00 to swap a fizzy drink for a glass of water.
🔥
StreaksCount consecutive goal days. Hit your goal and get a barrel roll and confetti.
♿️
Reduce MotionFollows your macOS accessibility setting and tones the animations down.

First launch

  1. Open Rayna-2.2.0.dmg and drag Rayna into Applications.
  2. Open Rayna from Applications. It's signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so it opens like any other app.
  3. Allow notifications if asked. Rayna lives in your menu bar (look for the 💧 and a percentage). There's no Dock icon.

Rayna is a universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Prefer to build it yourself? Instructions are on GitHub.
