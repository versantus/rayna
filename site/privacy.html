Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. This policy covers Rayna for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Nik Roberts / Versantus.

The short version

Rayna doesn't collect any data. Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What Rayna stores, and where

  • How much water you've logged today, your streak, and your settings (goal, glass size, reminder interval, quiet hours, run-day and evening-nudge options).
  • All of this is kept only on your device, in the app's local storage. On iPhone it's shared with Rayna's own Home Screen widget through an App Group on the same device.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Rayna has no server.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They don't go through any server, and you can switch them off in Rayna's Settings or in iOS/macOS Settings.

Third parties

Rayna has no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising, and doesn't share data with anyone. Apple may give developers aggregate, anonymous crash and usage stats if you've chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings.

Children

Rayna is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/versantus/rayna.

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
